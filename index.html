Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dispatch booking, rates, security, and scheduling.

How quickly will a rider arrive after I book?

For addresses within Ikeja, Maryland, and Yaba, average pickup arrival is 30 to 45 minutes from confirmation. For island pickups in Victoria Island or Lekki, arrival is typically 45 minutes depending on traffic conditions.

What is the difference between Standard and Priority Rush?

Standard Same-Day parcels are grouped into optimized corridor routes and delivered within 3 to 4 hours. Priority 90-Minute Rush assigns a dedicated rider solely to your consignment for immediate direct transit without detour.

How do I track my package in real-time?

Upon rider assignment, our dispatch desk sends you the rider name, verified phone number, and milestone timestamps via WhatsApp and SMS upon pickup and final delivery.

Do you offer payment on delivery or cash-on-delivery collection?

Yes. For verified retail merchants, we provide Cash-on-Delivery (COD) collection and bank transfer verification before releasing packages to end customers, with daily batch settlement.
